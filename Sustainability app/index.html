Dubai High Priority

Jumeirah Open Beach

A high-footfall public stretch where morning volunteer sweeps remove tidal debris and tourist litter before it washes out into Gulf waters.

🗑️ Target Debris: Single-use plastics, bottle caps, food wrappers
🚗 Facilities: Public parking, recycling bins, family walking paths

Dubai Active Monitoring

Kite Beach & Canal Edge

A lively water-sports hub with extensive shoreline. Ideal for targeted microplastic collection and community education along the dunes.

🗑️ Target Debris: Microplastics, beverage cups, straws, bottle caps
🚗 Facilities: Paved promenade, washrooms, shade areas
📍 Get Directions
Abu Dhabi Family Friendly

Abu Dhabi Corniche Beach

An expansive urban shoreline perfect for large school and corporate eco-days, volunteer orientations, and organized debris audits.

🗑️ Target Debris: Snack wrappers, beverage cans, plastic bottles
🚗 Facilities: Multiple parking plazas, shaded lawns, accessible pathways
📍 Get Directions
Abu Dhabi Protected Reserve

Saadiyat Marine & Coastal Reserve

A critical nesting ground for endangered Hawksbill turtles. Cleanups focus on clearing discarded marine ropes and tangled plastic flotsam.

🗑️ Target Debris: Abandoned fishing lines, ropes, floating plastics
🚗 Facilities: Designated eco-boardwalks, restricted dunes
📍 Get Directions
Sharjah Community Hub

Al Heera Beach

A major gathering hub along the Sharjah coastline where active youth volunteer squads coordinate weekend cleanups and promote waste separation.

🗑️ Target Debris: Beverage cans, takeaway containers, plastic bags
🚗 Facilities: Wide parking bays, seaside promenade, waste bins
📍 Get Directions
Sharjah High Priority

Al Khan Beach & Lagoon

Adjoining the historical lagoon and maritime heritage area, tidal shifts deposit concentrated marine debris that requires urgent attention.

🗑️ Target Debris: Fishing gear remnants, polystyrene foam, bottles
🚗 Facilities: Museum parking, maritime center, public access
📍 Get Directions
Ajman Active Monitoring

Ajman Public Beachfront

A central coastal stretch heavily visited by families. Routine volunteer patrols help prevent windblown debris from entering the water.

🗑️ Target Debris: Fast-food packaging, plastic bottles, straws
🚗 Facilities: Corniche parking, solar lighting, waste bins
📍 Get Directions
Ajman Ecology Hotspot

Al Zorah Mangrove Coast

A precious wetland and coastal mangrove forest sheltering migratory flamingos and coastal birds. Requires gentle, non-intrusive perimeter sweeps.

🗑️ Target Debris: Entangled plastic bags, styrofoam foam, drift bottles
🚗 Facilities: Nature reserve boundary, kayak launch, eco-trails
